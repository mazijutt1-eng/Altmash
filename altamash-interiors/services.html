What We Offer

Ceiling & Painting Services

From a single elegant ceiling to a full paint refresh, Altamash Interiors delivers professional workmanship with attention to every detail — for homes, offices and commercial spaces.

01 · Ceilings

Modern False Ceiling Designs

We design false ceilings that make a room feel larger, brighter and more considered. From simple perimeter bands to layered centre pieces, every design is planned around your furniture, lighting and the way you use the space.

  • Design ideas for living rooms, bedrooms, kitchens and offices
  • Clean joints, smooth surfaces and neat corners
  • Designs that balance detail with everyday calm
See Ceiling Designs
02 · Installation

Gypsum Ceilings & Installation

Gypsum is light, versatile and perfect for crisp modern ceilings. Our team measures, frames, fixes and finishes the surface so it is ready for paint or decorative treatment, with no rough edges left behind.

  • Accurate measurement and framing
  • Strong, tidy fixing with clean joint treatment
  • Suitable for new builds and renovations
Ask for a Quote
03 · Lighting

Cove & LED Lighting

The right lighting changes everything. We plan cove lights, profile lights, spotlights and recessed fittings so the glow is soft and even — highlighting the ceiling without creating harsh bright patches.

  • Warm, practical lighting plans for every room
  • Neat cable management and fitting placement
  • Works beautifully with gypsum and POP ceilings
View Lighting Ideas
04 · Painting

Interior & Exterior Painting

A good paint job protects your property and lifts its whole look. We prepare surfaces properly, use quality materials and apply even coats for a smooth, lasting finish inside and outside your home or office.

  • Surface preparation, priming and clean application
  • Colour advice to suit light, space and style
  • Residential, office and commercial properties
Discuss Colours
05 · Finishing

Wall Finishing

Walls that are level and well-finished make paint, panels and ceiling details look ten times better. We handle minor repairs, smoothing and texture work so the final surface is even and ready for its finish.

  • Crack filling and surface leveling
  • Smooth and textured finish options
  • Neat transitions between walls and ceilings

Decorative Paint Solutions
